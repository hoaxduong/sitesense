# Compare source-category businesses

The primary application ranks and compares individual businesses, filtered by
metro city cohort, broad business category, exact source category and dates.
This supersedes [the ZIP-area comparison decision](0001-compare-source-zip-areas.md).
Analysts need competitors in the same niche at their own locations; ZIP aggregates
hide that distinction and exclude small niches under area-size thresholds.

Keep activity at business/date/hour grain. A source tag selects whole listings;
it does not allocate their check-ins among tags. The map limits displayed circles
to top businesses while its scope chart and exports retain the matching population.
ZIP boundaries remain weather context and impose no business-count eligibility rule.

Covered dates without recorded activity are zero. Uncovered or failed reads remain
unavailable. One shared date range can add verified individual-business calendar
projections strictly after imported activity ends; unsupported estimates stay
unavailable. Recorded and projected values remain identified separately. Weather
boxes and activity totals share calendar periods, with unique station observations
in shared charts.

Keep snapshot profiles, observed activity and model projections distinguishable.
Neither weather associations nor historical calendar patterns establish causal
weather effects, complete market demand or new-store success. Earlier area models
and their evidence remain reproducible research rather than the current app model.
