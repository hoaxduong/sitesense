# Project features

These features define the intended scope of SiteSense AI. The app is currently a research workspace; application data ingestion, trained weather models, and location scoring remain future work.

1. **AI-Powered Store Location Recommendations:** Identify and rank potential store locations using Yelp business, review, and check-in data alongside climate and geographic information.

2. **Customer Activity Analysis:** Use Yelp check-ins as a proxy for customer activity to compare demand across locations, days, and seasons.

3. **Weather and Climate Impact Analysis:** Measure how weather events and climate anomalies relate to changes in local check-in activity.

4. **Demand Forecasting:** Predict location-level customer activity under typical seasonal conditions and potential weather disruptions.

5. **Location Comparison Dashboard:** Compare candidate sites by estimated demand, seasonality, weather sensitivity, and business-category trends.

6. **Explainable Recommendations:** Show which factors most influenced each location ranking and demand forecast, helping users make informed store-location decisions.

Check-ins represent an activity proxy, not unique customers or revenue. Weather relationships describe associations, and weather scenarios are model estimates rather than proof of causal effects.
